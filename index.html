Astrophotography FOV & Satellite Predictor Guide

1. Explicit Axis Scales on FOV View

The main FOV Sky View canvas now displays explicit numeric azimuth ($\Delta\text{Az}$) and altitude ($\Delta\text{Alt}$) offset tick marks in degrees along all four edges. This allows precise framing alignment relative to your telescope sensor grid.

2. Proportional Target Ellipse & Crosshairs

The deep sky target (Messier M1–M110) at the center of the canvas is rendered as a distinct circle or ellipse scaled dynamically to its actual cataloged major and minor angular dimensions (in arcminutes/degrees). The reticle crosshairs expand and contract proportionally with the object's physical dimensions.

3. High-Contrast Satellite Trail Colors

  • Bright Red (#FF1744): Satellite trajectory passes directly through the target object core.
  • Bright Orange (#FF6D00): Satellite passes across the sensor FOV frame, crossing background sky.
  • Electric Purple (#BF5AF2): Near-miss satellite pass within adjacent sky horizon.

4. Liang-Barsky Vector Truncation

All satellite vectors are clipped precisely at the boundary edges of the sensor FOV rectangle using Liang-Barsky clipping algorithms, ensuring trails never spill over onto axis tick labels or surrounding card areas.

5. Full Night Timeline Slider & Snap Button

Drag or resize the cyan session window box on the full night timeline bar to adjust your session start time and duration. The system automatically calculates the longest satellite-free window and provides a "Snap Here" button to quickly optimize your imaging schedule.
