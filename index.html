Astrophotography User Guide & Technical Manual

1. Sky View Field Canvas (FOV)

The top card visualizes your smart telescope sensor frame centered on your target. Bounding axes are strictly calibrated in Altitude (Alt) and Azimuth (Az) angular offsets from the target center (0°, 0°).

  • Red Trails (Through Target): Satellite vectors that pass directly inside the target's physical object boundary. Requires frame rejection or satellite removal stacking.
  • Orange Trails (Across Frame): Passes inside the sensor framing rectangle, contaminating background sky or star field.
  • Purple Trails (Near-Miss): Passes within 0.5° of the sensor edge, causing potential stray light or glare reflections.

2. Full Night Timeline & Shooting Window

The timeline displays dusk-to-dawn satellite density ticks. You can adjust your session shooting window in two ways:

  • Interactive Drag Window: Drag the central cyan body to slide across the night, or drag the left/right handles to zoom/stretch the duration.
  • Mint Green Highlight: Automatically detects the longest uninterrupted gap between satellite passes during the night. Click "Snap Here" to lock your session to this cleanest interval.

3. Efficiency & Frame Calculation

Using your selected Sub-Exposure length (10s to 300s), the app calculates exact sub-frame corruption. If a satellite trail enters the FOV during a exposure, that frame is flagged as corrupted.

4. GPS & Celestial Coordinates

Click the restored 📍 GPS Button in the top header to fetch your exact latitude and longitude for precise real-time satellite pass trajectory calculation.

Astrophotography Satellite Trail Predictor - User Guide

1. Timeline Controller & Zoom Window

Drag the center of the blue session window on the timeline to slide your planned astrophotography session across the night. Drag the left and right handle grips to adjust session duration (from 10 minutes up to the full night). Use the Quick-Snap buttons to jump to pre-calculated windows like "Longest Clean Stretch".

2. Target Selection (Messier Catalog M1 - M110)

Select any object from M1 to M110. The FOV Canvas automatically renders a proportional target crosshair and target outline centered at (0°, 0°) based on cataloged angular dimensions (e.g., M31 Andromeda: 190' x 60', M42 Orion: 65' x 60').

3. Pure Altitude & Azimuth Coordinates

All coordinate representations utilize real-time Horizontal Coordinates (Altitude ° above horizon and Azimuth ° true north). Canvas axes represent relative offsets (ΔAzimuth horizontal, ΔAltitude vertical) in degrees.

4. Trajectory Trail Types & Edge Clipping

Satellite vector trails are cleanly truncated at plot limits using Liang-Barsky vector edge clipping. Trails are categorized into:

  • Through Target: Pass directly across the target object reticle radius (< 15' offset).
  • Across Frame: Pass inside your telescope's sensor Field of View.
  • Near-Miss: Pass within 1.0° outside your FOV.

5. Sub-Exposures & Corrupted Frames Calculation

By adjusting your sub-exposure duration (e.g., 60 seconds), the system calculates how many single frames will contain satellite streak artifacts versus clean frames, providing an exact Imaging Efficiency % forecast.
