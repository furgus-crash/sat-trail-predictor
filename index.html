Astrophotography Tool Guide & Manual

1. Telescope Presets & FOV

Choose your smart telescope or camera optical system from the preset selector (e.g. Seestar S50, Dwarf 3, Vespera series, Stellina, Unistellar eVscope). Selecting a preset automatically calculates sensor resolution, focal length, and exact Field of View (FOV) dimensions in degrees and arcminutes.

2. Messier Target Selection

Select any deep sky object from the Messier catalog (M1 to M110). Target Right Ascension (RA) and Declination (Dec) are populated instantly. Real-time altitude and azimuth visibility are calculated based on your location and chosen date.

3. Interactive FOV View Plot & Legend Toggles

The FOV View renders your telescope frame with central target crosshairs and simulated satellite trails passing through or near your camera frame.

  • Through Target (Red): Passes directly over or within core center zone of target.
  • Across Frame (Orange): Passes across the rectangular FOV frame.
  • Near-miss (Purple): Passes within 1.5x FOV boundary.

Use the interactive legend toggle buttons above the FOV View to enable or disable specific trail types to unclutter your display!

4. Interactive Night Timeline

The Astronomical Timeline illustrates night twilight phases (Sunset, Civil, Nautical, Astronomical twilight, Deep Night).

  • Slide Window: Click/touch and drag the main highlighted session window to shift your observation start time earlier or later in the night.
  • Stretch Window: Click/touch and drag the left or right edge handles to expand or shrink session duration.
