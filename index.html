Astrophotography FOV & Satellite User Guide

How satellite trail prediction, sub-exposure length, and timeline controls work

📍 GPS & Location Setup

Click the 📍 Use GPS Location button to instantly fetch your exact latitude and longitude via standard browser geolocation. If location access is blocked, the application gracefully defaults to Minneapolis, MN (44.9778° N, 93.2650° W).

↔️ Sliding & Stretching the Session Timeline

The timeline window allows drag-and-drop planning:

  • Slide Session: Click and drag the middle sky-blue box left or right to change the session start time while keeping duration constant.
  • Resize Handles: Drag the left edge to change start time and stretch duration. Drag the right edge to extend or trim the total imaging session.

🌌 FOV Canvas & Target Dimensions

The Sky View canvas scales relative angular dimensions (-3.55° to +3.55° Azimuth and -2.00° to +2.00° Altitude). The Messier target (M1 through M110) displays its true angular size in arcminutes overlaid with target reticles and color-coded trail classifications:

  • Vivid Red (#FF1744): Satellite passes directly through the galaxy/nebula target ellipse.
  • Bright Orange (#FF6D00): Satellite crosses inside the camera sensor FOV.
  • Electric Purple (#BF5AF2): Near-miss pass within a 1° buffer zone outside sensor boundaries.

⏱️ Sub-Exposure Length & Stack Corruption

Short sub-exposures (e.g. 10s to 300s) isolate satellite trails to fewer total frames. Stacking software (Siril, PixInsight, DeepSkyStacker) uses Sigma Rejection to discard satellite trails easily if corrupted frames remain below ~10% of total stack size.
