Astrophotography Satellite Predictor Guide

1. Sky Target & Optics Field of View

Select your astro-imaging target object (e.g. M31 Andromeda) and smart telescope preset (DWARF II, Seestar S50, Vespera, eVscope). The simulator scales the Field of View (FOV) rectangle precisely according to sensor and optical focal length, vector-clipping satellite orbital trails to match what your camera sensor records.

2. Photography Session Window & Longest Clean Window

Adjust your planned imaging window start time and total exposure duration using the sliders or duration buttons. Click "Select Longest Clean Window" to automatically snap your session start and duration to the single largest gap tonight where zero satellites cross your FOV!

3. Interactive Timeline & Pass Classification

The timeline illustrates satellite pass density from Astronomical Dusk to Dawn. Trails are color coded:

  • Target Direct (Red): Satellite cuts through center target interest zone.
  • Across Frame (Orange): Satellite passes through sensor frame boundary.
  • Near-miss (Purple): Satellite passes within 1.5 degrees of FOV frame edge.
  • Mint Green Overlay: Highlighted continuous gap with zero frame-corrupting passes.

4. Frame Corruption & Sub-exposure Calculations

Based on sub-exposure length (e.g. 30 seconds) plus readout overhead (e.g. 5 seconds), the engine calculates exactly how many individual sub-frames will contain satellite streaks. Subtracting corrupt subs yields your true clean integration time and overall session efficiency percentage.
