Astrophotography Tool User Guide

Section 1: Session & Target Controls

Select your observation date, location coordinates, telescope optical preset, and Messier target object (M1 through M110). Adjust sub-exposure lengths between 10s and 300s to analyze how frame duration affects trail corruption risks.

Section 2: Telescope Field of View (FOV)

The canvas simulates your exact field of view in Altitude and Azimuth degrees. Target objects are rendered to scale using cataloged angular dimensions. Trail vectors are mathematically clipped to the viewport border using the Liang-Barsky algorithm.

  • Through Target: Trail crosses directly through the target footprint.
  • Across Frame: Trail crosses the telescope FOV but misses the target object.
  • Near Miss: Trail passes within 0.5 degrees of the FOV border.

Section 3: Full Night Timeline & Window Snapping

The timeline spans from Astronomical Dusk to Astronomical Dawn. Color-coded satellite crossing bars are drawn along the timeline at their exact pass times. Drag or stretch the cyan box to adjust your active session window. Click "Snap Window to Longest Clear Gap" to immediately jump your capture session to the cleanest window of the night.

Section 4: Integration Efficiency Calculations

Calculates total sub-exposures captured during the session ($\text{Duration} / \text{SubExposureLength}$). Sub-exposures that overlap with satellite crossings are flagged as corrupted, providing exact integration loss time and efficiency percentages.

Section 5: Satellites Active in Session Table

Lists every satellite crossing your sky region during the selected window. Click any satellite row or details icon to open orbital telemetry metadata, including NORAD ID, operator, launch date, and velocity.
