Astrophotography Trail Predictor Guide

How to plan target sessions and minimize satellite trail interference

1. Session Planning & Location Setup

Enter your Target Date, Start Time, and Session Duration in the top control bar. Click Use GPS to fetch your exact latitude and longitude, or type custom coordinates directly.

2. Equipment & Target Field of View (FOV)

Select your smart scope or optical tube preset (ZWO Seestar S50, Dwarf II, Vespera II, eVscope 2). Next, pick a Messier target (M1 to M110). The simulator calculates the precise field of view dimensions in degrees and projects target extent on the canvas.

3. Sub-Exposure Selection & Rejection Strategy

Choosing shorter sub-exposure times (e.g. 10s–30s) isolates satellite trails to fewer frames, allowing rejection algorithms (Sigma Clip / Winsorized / Kappa-Sigma) to discard trail artifacts without losing large chunks of light integration time.

4. Reading the FOV Canvas & Timeline

Direct hits cross the inner sensor frame. Purple dashed lines indicate Near-Miss Warnings (satellites passing near the FOV boundary). Drag the session window on the full-night timeline to find the quietest shooting window with minimal Starlink constellation passes.
